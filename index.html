How to qualify for the 2026–27 National Championship
Defending champion NM Alfred A. Awe is joined by seven more players in a classical round robin for the title. The ten spots are filled through: Belize Grand Swiss 2027 winner · Belizean Crown Cup winner · Belize Chess Classic 2026 and 2027 winners · top finishers on the Championship Circuit · and the highest-rated active player (min. 30 rapid/classical games between Feb 2026 and Sep 2027).

Championship format & time controls
Finals are now a single classical round robin (no title match) at 90 + 30 with notation. The Junior Championship runs annually from 2026 — a five-round Swiss at 30 + 2 for players under 16 on 1 January; ties for the podium are decided by blitz playoff.
How the Championship Circuit points work
Points are P = B × k × w. Basic points B: 1st = 11 down to 6th = 4. Strength factor k = (TAR − 1350) / 100, where TAR is the average rating of the top six players. Weighting w: classical 2.0, Grand Swiss / Crown Cup 1.6, rapid 1.2, national blitz 1.0, blitz 0.7. A player's season score is their best seven results.

About the federation

The Belize Chess Federation is the national governing body for chess in Belize, working alongside the Belize National Youth Chess Foundation and the Seniors Chess Association to bring the game to every district. Its founding motto still guides the federation's work today.

"For the benefit of all people."

Founded in 1987 by Ricardo Aguilar, the federation was reactivated in 2008 after a quiet decade, and gained full membership in FIDE, the world chess governing body, in 2022 — alongside Dominica, Niger, and St. Vincent and the Grenadines.

The Belize National Youth Chess Foundation, co-founded by Ian and Ella Anderson in 2007 and headquartered at Caves Branch Jungle Lodge in Belmopan, hosts the annual National Chess Olympiad every May and has grown the game to more than fifty clubs nationwide.

The chess capital of Central America

Belize at a glance

From Maya ruins in Cayo to the cayes and the Caribbean coast — a small country with a big chess story.

One country · six districts · fifty clubs

Independent since 1981, Belize is the only English-speaking nation in Central America. Its flag's shield features two woodcutters and a mahogany tree — the 50 leaves on the wreath stand for the six districts that together feed Belize's national chess circuit.
