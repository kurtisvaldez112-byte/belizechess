About the federation

The Belize Chess Federation is the national governing body for chess in Belize, working alongside the Belize National Youth Chess Foundation and the Seniors Chess Association to bring the game to every district. Its founding motto still guides the federation's work today.

"For the benefit of all people."

Founded in 1987 by Ricardo Aguilar, the federation was reactivated in 2008 after a quiet decade, and gained full membership in FIDE, the world chess governing body, in 2022 — alongside Dominica, Niger, and St. Vincent and the Grenadines.

The Belize National Youth Chess Foundation, co-founded by Ian and Ella Anderson in 2007 and headquartered at Caves Branch Jungle Lodge in Belmopan, hosts the annual National Chess Olympiad every May and has grown the game to more than fifty clubs nationwide.
